FAQ

Good questions

Which apps does it work with?

Any PDF annotation app: GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf, Xodo, Samsung Notes and more. The savings kit is made for printing on US Letter paper.

Is this a physical product?

No. You'll receive a digital PDF download. Nothing is shipped.

How do I use the hyperlinks?

Open the PDF in your app's read or view mode and tap a tab. In GoodNotes, switch off the pen first, then tap.

Can I reuse pages?

Yes. In most apps you can duplicate a page from the page thumbnails, so you never run out.

Refunds?

Because files are delivered instantly, sales are final. If something isn't working, get in touch and we'll make it right.
